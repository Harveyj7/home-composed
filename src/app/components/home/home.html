What is the Home Composed Song Contest?

Original songs written by Eurovision fans from across Europe compete in a friendly songwriting competition, judged by the competitors themselves, guest juries, and an element of public voting.

First devised in 1991, this is the 35th edition of the contest. After Eurovision, we believe it is the longest-running pan-European songwriting contest in existence.
