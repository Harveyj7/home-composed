Privacy Policy - The Home Composed Song Contest 2025

The Home Composed Song Contest 2025 ("the Contest") is organised by Philip Andrew Brook ("the Organiser").

1. Information Collected

Participants may provide personal information, such as name, email address, and social media or WhatsApp contact details, when communicating with the Organiser by email, Facebook Messenger, or WhatsApp.

2. Use of Information

Personal information will be used solely for the purposes of organising and administering the Contest, including communication with participants before, during, and after the event.

3. Data Sharing

Your personal information will not be shared with any third parties, nor used for marketing or unrelated purposes.

4. Data Retention

Personal information will be retained only for as long as necessary to manage the Contest and maintain appropriate records of participation.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about how your information is handled, please contact the Organiser, Philip Andrew Brook, via the same communication channels: email, Facebook Messenger, or WhatsApp.
