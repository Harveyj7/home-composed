Contest history

A Eurovision-style contest with more than three decades of songs

The Home Composed Song Contest is an annual online competition for non-professional songwriters who are also fans of the Eurovision Song Contest.

First held in 1991, the aim of Home Composed is simple: to give fans who write their own songs the opportunity to take part in a Eurovision-style contest.

36 countries represented
34th edition approaching
700th entry milestone

Over the years, 36 different countries have been represented in the competition, from Portugal to Ukraine and most points in between. As the contest approaches its 34th edition, we believe that it is the longest-running pan-European song contest other than Eurovision itself. The competition also reaches a milestone with its 700th entry.

The compositions go head-to-head in a friendly competition, culminating in an exciting voting presentation. The competitors also form juries who, along with the public and invited judges, decide the eventual winner of the competition.
